Account deletion (English)

Valhalla Chronicle · Developer: 무쏘오 · Contact: silvermuso@gmail.com

If you signed in to Valhalla Chronicle with Google, you can delete your account and your cloud save.

In the game: open the menu (three lines, top left) in town → Settings, tap Delete account (계정 삭제) and confirm. Your account and cloud save are deleted immediately.

If you can't use the game: email silvermuso@gmail.com with the subject "Valhalla Chronicle account deletion" and the Google account email you signed in with. We delete it promptly and reply.

Deleted: account information (Firebase user ID, Google account name and email) and the cloud save (full save, save time, summary). Kept: game data on your device (remove it by uninstalling or Settings → Delete records) and Google Play purchase records, which are held by Google Play. Nothing else is retained after deletion.
