Account deletion (English)

One-Hand Valhalla · Developer: 무쏘오 · Contact: silvermuso@gmail.com

If you signed in to One-Hand Valhalla with Google, you can delete your account, your cloud save and your ranking records.

In the game: open the menu (three lines, top left) in town → Settings, tap Delete account (계정 삭제) and confirm. Your account, cloud save and ranking records are deleted immediately.

If you can't use the game: email silvermuso@gmail.com with the subject "One-Hand Valhalla account deletion" and the Google account email you signed in with. We delete it promptly and reply.

Deleted: account information (Firebase user ID, Google account name and email) the cloud save (full save, save time, summary) and ranking records (name, floor, time, level, power, gear, skills). Reports about a player are deleted once handled. Kept: game data on your device (remove it by uninstalling or Settings → Delete records) and Google Play purchase records, which are held by Google Play. Nothing else is retained after deletion.
