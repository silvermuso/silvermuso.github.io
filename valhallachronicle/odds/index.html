Drop rates (English)

Spirit summon (100 Rune Crystals, 10× for 900, or one free summon per day by watching an ad): each of the eight spirits has an equal 12.5% chance on every summon. A 10× summon is ten single summons with no guarantee or pity. A new spirit joins at Lv1; duplicates become level-up material (65 copies to reach Lv10); duplicates of a fully collected spirit are refunded as 10 Rune Crystals. The same rates are shown in the game via the "확률" (rates) button.

Gear enhancement (+1 to +10; Rune Stones, Holy Rune Stones and Protection Stones can also be bought with Rune Crystals). Success chance by target step: +1 and +2 100%, +3 85%, +4 70%, +5 55%, +6 42%, +7 32%, +8 22%, +9 14%, +10 8%. A Holy Rune Stone adds 10 percentage points when enhancing to +6 or higher. Failing at +6 or higher drops the item one step unless a Protection Stone is used. Items are never destroyed. After 6 failures at the same step (any step below 100%), the next try at that step always succeeds. The game's enhance window also shows the chance for each try.
