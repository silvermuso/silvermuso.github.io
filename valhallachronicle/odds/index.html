Drop rates (English)

Spirit summon (100 Rune Crystals, 10× for 900, or one free summon per day by watching an ad): each of the eight spirits has an equal 12.5% chance on every summon. A 10× summon is ten single summons with no guarantee or pity. A new spirit joins at Lv1; duplicates become level-up material (65 copies to reach Lv10); duplicates of a fully collected spirit are refunded as 10 Rune Crystals. The same rates are shown in the game via the "확률" (rates) button.
