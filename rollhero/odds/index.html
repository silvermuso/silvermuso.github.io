Drop rates (English)

The tables above list the exact probability of every pet, hero and equipment rarity from the in-game shop draws (rarities: 일반 Common · 고급 Uncommon · 희귀 Rare · 영웅 Epic · 전설 Legendary · 신화 Mythic). Pet and hero draws guarantee a Legendary-or-better result within 50 pet draws / 60 hero draws (pity), and every 10-draw contains at least one result of the rarity stated above. The same rates are shown in the game via the "확률" (rates) button on each draw.
